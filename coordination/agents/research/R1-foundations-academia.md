# R1: Foundations and Academia

## Mission

Build the conceptual evidence base for Chapters 1-4, 9, 12, 15, 17, and 19-22. Explain the history and mechanics needed to connect classical agents, modern language models, planning, memory, multi-agent systems, and evaluation without turning the book into an ML textbook.

## Research questions

- Which definitions cleanly distinguish agents, workflows, assistants, and automation?
- Which classical agent architectures remain useful for modern system design?
- What minimum transformer, probability, embedding, and inference concepts support sound engineering decisions?
- What evidence supports or challenges planning, reflection, memory, and multi-agent patterns?
- Which evaluation methods are reliable enough for production decisions?

# R9: Regulation and Governance

## Mission

Map current official standards and regulatory material to engineering controls while avoiding jurisdiction-specific legal conclusions.

## Research questions

- Which official frameworks define risk management, transparency, testing, human oversight, incident reporting, privacy, and accessibility obligations?
- How should engineers translate those requirements into evidence, controls, and release gates?
- Which claims vary by jurisdiction, system classification, or enforcement date?
- What requires qualified legal, privacy, accessibility, or compliance review?

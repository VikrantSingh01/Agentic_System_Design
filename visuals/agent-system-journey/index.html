    Where the help-center picture stops matching

    • The model is not a person and does not understand or know. It predicts likely tokens and can be confidently wrong.
    • The Runtime reconstructs bounded context; it does not remember naturally. Cross-run memory is separate, evaluated, opt-in, and off by default.
    • Approval is narrower than a human signature: it is bound to an exact payload, destination, policy version, and expiry.
    • Software workers do not exercise independent judgment. They exchange structured requests and results within fixed authority.
    • The system cannot improvise past its rulebook. Authority never increases at runtime; anything not explicitly allowed is denied.
